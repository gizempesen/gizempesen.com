Electronic Monitoring Systems

As a member of the DevOps team for the Electronic Monitoring System (EIS) project at the General Directorate of Security and the Ministry of Justice in Ankara, Turkey, I actively contributed to the project's success. Utilizing Gitlab for version control and managing CI/CD processes, we established an integrated pipeline that significantly accelerated software development, testing, and deployment phases. Employing on-premise Kubernetes orchestrated by Rancher, we automated infrastructure management and scaling capabilities. This initiative not only optimized development workflows but also laid the foundation for a reliable and efficient software delivery infrastructure for the Electronic Monitoring System project. The experience underscores the effectiveness of strong team collaboration and the application of modern DevOps practices in the successful execution of public safety projects.

BLC Platform as a Service (PAAS)

BLC Platform as a Service (PaaS) project represents a comprehensive initiative where I played a key role in implementing and managing various services to facilitate a robust and scalable environment. Leveraging tools like Kubespray for Kubernetes orchestration, Docker for containerization, and integrating essential data services such as Hadoop, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB, we created a dynamic and flexible platform. The integration of Kubespray allowed us to efficiently manage containerized applications and services, ensuring seamless deployment, scaling, and orchestration. Docker facilitated the containerization of applications, enhancing portability and resource utilization. Furthermore, the inclusion of data services like Hadoop, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB enabled the platform to support diverse data processing and storage needs. This PaaS project has not only streamlined development and deployment processes but has also provided a foundation for future scalability and innovation within the BLC ecosystem.
